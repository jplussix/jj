```
[Generation Goal]

[Reference Material Roles]
@Image 1 defines Park Mansu's face, hairstyle, build and clothing. Do not use the grey background or the panel layout.
@Image 2 defines Executive Choi's face, hairstyle, build and clothing. Do not use the grey background or the panel layout.
@Image 3 defines the logistics office interior with glass walls showing forklifts outside, a desk, paper form, and pen. @Image 3 is a style reference only, not a fixed keyframe.

[Subjects and Relationships]
Park Mansu corresponds to @Image 1; Executive Choi corresponds to @Image 2. Executive Choi sits behind a wooden desk facing forward; Park Mansu sits opposite him across the desk. [Event Script]
Stage 1 - The refusal: Opening state: Park Mansu looks down at the document on the desk. Executive Choi leans slightly forward and says in Korean, {부장님, 저도 마음이 안 좋습니다.} Park Mansu looks up, pleading politely in Korean, {상무님. 우리 애 취직할 때까지만요. 일 년만.} Executive Choi shakes his head calmly and says in Korean, {본사 결재가 어제 끝났어요.} End state: Executive Choi sits back with a neutral expression.
Stage 2 - Hesitation and signing: Park Mansu picks up the pen, hesitates and sets it down, then picks it up again. He says in Korean, {제가 여기서 이십칠 년입니다.} Executive Choi replies smoothly in Korean, {그래서 위로금도 넉넉히 넣었습니다.} Park Mansu signs the paper with a trembling hand, unclasps the locker key hanging around his neck, and places it on top of the document. End state: Park Mansu rests his hands on his knees, looking down.

[Visual and Camera Treatment]
Stage 1 begins with a medium two-shot across the desk at eye level. Stage 2 slowly pushes in to a close-up of Park Mansu's hands picking up the pen, signing the document, and dropping the locker key onto the paper. Bright indoor fluorescent light mixed with sunlight from the glass wall.

[Audio]
<muffled sound of forklifts outside glass>, <pen scratching on paper>, <metal key clicking on desk>. Executive Choi speaks Korean in a polished, smooth, businesslike voice of a man in his late forties.

[Maintain Consistency]
Keep Park Mansu's and Executive Choi's faces and clothing identical to their references; keep the glass wall and moving forklifts in the background behind Executive Choi.
```
